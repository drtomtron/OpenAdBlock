OpenAdBlock is ready

Ads, pop-ups and trackers are being blocked on every site. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to configure first.

What is on by default

  • EasyList for advertising.
  • EasyPrivacy for trackers and analytics beacons.
  • ABP anti-circumvention for sites that try to defeat blockers.
  • A regional supplement, if one matches your browser language.

Acceptable Ads is off. OpenAdBlock has no allowlisting deals with anyone, so nothing is let through unless you ask for it.

Worth knowing

  • Click the toolbar button to switch blocking off for one site, or to pause everywhere.
  • Use Block an element in the popup to hide something a list missed.
  • Cookie notices, social buttons and other annoyance lists are available in settings.
  • Nothing about your browsing leaves your computer. There is no account and no telemetry.
