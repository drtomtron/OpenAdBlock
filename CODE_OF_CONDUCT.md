# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Discuss code, not the person who wrote it. Assume the other person is
acting in good faith and has context you do not.

## What is expected

- Keep criticism about the work: what it does, what it breaks, how it could be clearer.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change that does not fit the project's goals, and
  that contributors may decline to do more work than they signed up for.
- Respect the privacy stance of this project in discussion as well as in code: do not ask
  for telemetry, user counts or anything that would identify users.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, personal attacks, or demeaning comments about anyone's identity, background
  or ability.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or deliberate bad faith.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages and any other project
space, and to public conduct that reasonably reflects on the project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to the maintainers privately through a
[GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/drtomtron/OpenAdBlock/security/advisories/new),
which is the only private channel the project has today, or by contacting a maintainer
directly. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, or block accounts, in proportion to
what happened. Where a report is about a maintainer, the remaining maintainers handle it.
